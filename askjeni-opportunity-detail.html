About the role

Peach Payments is South Africa's leading payments infrastructure company, processing billions of Rands in transactions annually for merchants across Africa. Our engineering team builds the systems that keep online commerce running.

As a Junior Software Engineer you'll join a small, cross-functional squad working on our merchant-facing dashboard and API integrations. You'll be shipping real code in your first week — we don't do "grunt work" probation periods.

You'll be working on:
  • React-based merchant dashboard features
  • Node.js and Python API services
  • Payment webhook processing and event handling
  • Internal tooling to help the support team resolve merchant queries faster
